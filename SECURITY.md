# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for security problems. Report them privately through
GitHub: open the **Security** tab of this repository and choose **Report a vulnerability**.

Include the version of Meshwork, your operating system, and the steps to reproduce. We aim to
acknowledge reports within a few working days and will keep you informed until a fix ships.

## Supported versions

Security fixes are made for the latest released version only. Direct downloads update
themselves; Mac App Store builds update through the App Store.

## Scope

Meshwork talks to devices on networks you choose to scan and parses what they return (web
pages, certificates, SSH greetings, mDNS, SSDP, ONVIF and SNMP data). Problems where a device on
the network can crash, stall or take control of the app are in scope and especially welcome.
